Transparent Criteria

Eligibility Criteria & Documentation

Clear standards for both salaried and self-employed applicants. No hidden conditions.

For Salaried Applicants

Eligibility Thresholds:

  • Age: 21 to 58 years at the time of application
  • Employment: Minimum 6 months continuous service
  • Minimum Monthly Income: ₹20,000 net take-home salary
  • Salary Mode: Direct bank account credit (cash salary not accepted)
  • Credit Profile: CIBIL / credit bureau score of 650+ preferred

Required Documents:

  • PAN Card (Mandatory for tax identity verification)
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport (Proof of identity & current address)
  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months salary account bank statements (PDF format)

For Self-Employed Individuals

Eligibility Thresholds:

  • Age: 23 to 65 years at the time of application
  • Business Vintage: Minimum 2 years of active business operations
  • Minimum Annual Net Income: ₹3,00,000 as per verified ITR
  • Banking Activity: Consistent operational current/savings account
  • Credit Profile: CIBIL / credit bureau score of 675+ preferred

Required Documents:

  • PAN Card of Individual / Firm
  • Aadhaar / Proof of Business Address
  • Last 2 years Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Computation of Income
  • Last 6 to 12 months primary operative bank statements
  • Business Registration Proof (GST Certificate, Shop & Est., or Udyam)

Borrow Safely

Responsible Borrowing Commitment

Personal loans are financial commitments. We encourage every borrower to evaluate their repayment capacity carefully.

  • Assess Your Debt-to-Income Ratio (FOIR):

    Ensure that your cumulative loan EMIs (including car, credit card, or existing loans) do not exceed 50% of your take-home pay.

  • Avoid Borrowing for Speculative Activities:

    Never take personal loans to fund volatile market speculation, gambling, or unregulated schemes.

  • Maintain an Emergency Buffer:

    Borrow only what you require, ensuring you maintain a reserve for unexpected medical or household obligations.
