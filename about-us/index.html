Regulatory Trust & Lending Service Provider (LSP) Disclosure

ClickMyLoan operates as a Digital Lending Platform and Lending Service Provider (LSP) in accordance with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Guidelines on Digital Lending. ClickMyLoan does not directly issue loans or collect deposits. All loan sanctions, interest rate determinations, and credit disbursements are executed exclusively by our regulated partner Banks and RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs): [Regulated Lending Partners & NBFC Certificate Details to be confirmed by ClickMyLoan team].

Zero Advance Fee Policy: ClickMyLoan strictly maintains a zero advance fee policy. We never solicit advance payments, cash transfers, or security deposits for loan approvals. Applicable processing fee (8.99% + GST) is deducted directly from the sanctioned loan disbursal as specified in the Key Fact Statement (KFS). Foreclosure charges are 0%.

Statutory Look-Up Period: Borrowers are entitled to a mandatory cooling-off / look-up period during which they can exit the digital loan without paying any penalty, as mandated under RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.
