Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant, transparent answers to questions regarding eligibility, interest rates, documentation, regulatory policies, and security.

ClickMyLoan is a modern digital lending platform and Lending Service Provider (LSP) providing short-term personal loans up to ₹5,00,000 to eligible Indian borrowers. We facilitate transparent digital credit in strict compliance with the RBI Digital Lending Directions in partnership with regulated Banks and RBI-registered NBFCs.

Eligible borrowers can receive loan sanctions up to ₹5,00,000. Your specific sanctioned amount is determined based on your monthly income, debt-to-income ratio (FOIR), credit assessment, and partner lender credit policies.

The maximum loan amount available through ClickMyLoan is ₹5,00,000, subject to borrower eligibility, credit appraisal, and verification.

The applicable interest rate ranges from 0.2% to 1% per day. The exact rate depends on your risk assessment, credit profile, and tenure, and is clearly disclosed in your Key Fact Statement (KFS) before loan acceptance.

The processing fee is 8.99% of the sanctioned loan amount + applicable GST (18%). This fee is deducted directly from the disbursed loan amount. ClickMyLoan never asks for upfront or advance fees.

You can apply 100% digitally through our website: (1) Click "Apply Now" or "Check Your Eligibility", (2) Enter your mobile number and basic profile details, (3) Complete digital KYC and bank verification, and (4) Review your approved loan offer.

Standard documents include your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card (for digital paperless e-KYC), bank account details, and income/employment verification (such as bank statement or salary slip). Documents may vary based on eligibility and lender requirements.

The journey consists of 4 digital steps: (1) Apply online with basic contact details, (2) Verify identity and income paperlessly, (3) Review approved terms in the Key Fact Statement (KFS), and (4) E-sign your agreement to receive funds directly into your verified bank account.

Eligibility is determined based on customer profile, regular income, credit assessment, age (typically 21–58 years), KYC verification, and applicable lender policies.

The online application takes approximately 2 minutes to submit. Upon complete document verification and approval by our regulated lending partners, eligible applicants receive direct account disbursal as per the applicable process.

Repayment is calculated based on the sanctioned principal amount, the applicable daily interest rate (between 0.2% and 1% per day), and the agreed tenure. A clear breakdown is provided in the Key Fact Statement before acceptance.
